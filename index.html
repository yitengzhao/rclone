个人 Google Drive 上传工具

这是一个供本人使用的 Google Drive 文件上传配置。它使用开源工具 rclone，将本人选择的文件从私人服务器上传到本人授权的 Google Drive 账号。此网站仅说明该用途，不提供公开上传或账号登录服务。

授权仅用于按照账号持有人的指令执行文件传输和核对。网站本身不会读取 Google Drive 文件。

Personal Google Drive uploader

This is a personal-use Google Drive upload setup. It uses the open-source rclone tool to transfer files selected by the account owner from a private server to the owner's authorized Google Drive account. This static website explains the setup; it does not provide a public upload or sign-in service.
